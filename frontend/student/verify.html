Your location and privacy

  • Your browser asks for location permission only when you tap Verify my location. Nothing is read in the background.
  • trayUP reads your location once to check that you're within the canteen area. The coordinates are used for that check and then discarded.
  • We keep only the rounded distance from the canteen and your GPS accuracy, for an audit trail. These records are deleted after a limited retention period.
  • Codes change every few seconds, and each verification allows one order for three minutes. This makes a photographed code much less useful. No location check is perfect, so staff can still review orders.
  • We don't use your IP address to decide where you are.
